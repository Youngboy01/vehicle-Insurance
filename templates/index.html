Step 1 · Score one customer

Estimate response propensity

Enter one eligible customer record to estimate how strongly the model prioritizes that customer for a cross-sell campaign.

What you get

A score from 0 to 1. Higher scores indicate a stronger prioritization signal compared with other customers. The score is not a guaranteed probability or a business approval decision.

What do these fields mean?

Use the values from your customer or policy system. The form starts with an example record; replace the example values before scoring.

Customer ID Your unique customer identifier. Use the same ID used by your customer system; enter a whole number from 1 to 9,007,199,254,740,991.
Gender The gender value recorded for the customer: Male or Female.
Age The customer's current age in years. The accepted range is 18 to 100.
Driving license Choose Yes when the customer has a driving license; otherwise choose No.
Region code The geographic region code from your source system. It is not automatically a postal code.
Previously insured Choose Yes when the customer already has vehicle insurance; otherwise choose No.
Vehicle age Select the age bracket that matches the customer's vehicle.
Vehicle damage Choose Yes when existing vehicle damage is recorded; otherwise choose No.
Annual premium The current annual policy premium in rupees. Enter a positive number.
Policy sales channel The numeric sales-channel code from your source system, not the channel name.
Customer vintage The number of days since the customer relationship or policy record began.

Do not enter the historical response outcome, campaign result, or any field that was created after the campaign. Those values would leak the answer into the scoring request.

Customer result
—
Higher scores indicate stronger campaign priority.

Use this value to compare customers and decide who should be considered first for outreach. It does not predict a guaranteed response and does not make an eligibility, pricing, or underwriting decision.

Step 2 · Build a campaign list

Prioritize a campaign audience

Use this when you have more eligible customers than your campaign team can contact. The service scores every record and returns the highest-priority customers within the capacity you choose.

How ranking works
  1. Provide eligible pre-campaign customer records.
  2. Choose a percentage or a maximum number of customers.
  3. Review the highest-scoring records in campaign order.

Equal scores are ordered by ascending customer ID. Customers outside the selected capacity are lower priority, not rejected.

Advanced input: paste customer records

The current API accepts a JSON array. Each record must contain the required customer ID (a whole number from 1 to 9,007,199,254,740,991) and the ten eligible scoring fields described in the field guide above. Do not include the historical response outcome or other campaign results.

Add customer records and choose a capacity to see the recommended campaign list.
